# Example interaction

Using the fictional profile in [example-persona.md](example-persona.md):

**User:** I have two hours this evening. What should I work on?

**Persona-aware response:** Spend 75 minutes improving one portfolio project, 30 minutes writing a short case study explaining your design decisions, and 15 minutes saving screenshots and updating your portfolio notes. That creates a concrete result without requiring a paid tool.

**Why this follows the skill:**
- It uses the confirmed goal of improving a design portfolio.
- It respects the preference for practical, low-cost work.
- It does not invent obligations, family circumstances, or a fixed evening routine.
- The proposed schedule is advice for this situation, not a claim about the persona's normal habits.
